Section 01

Overview & Policy Scope

At Sunkiran Dimex, we are committed to delivering elite software engineering, high-converting digital portals, automated marketing systems, and high-ROAS ad campaigns for businesses across India and globally.

Due to the bespoke nature of customized software development, cloud infrastructure setup, proprietary digital assets, and allocated engineering sprint hours, all payments made are generally non-refundable once work has commenced, except under the explicit and transparent conditions defined in this policy.

Transparent Partnership Guarantee: Every commercial engagement is governed by a detailed Statement of Work (SOW) or digital agreement that defines deliverables, milestones, and scope boundaries before any invoice is issued.
Section 02

Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds (full or prorated) may be considered and approved under the following specific circumstances:

  • Pre-Initiation Cancellation: If a client requests formal cancellation in writing prior to project kickoff, resource assignment, or technical repository staging.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Transactions: In the event of an accidental double charge or billing system discrepancy verified by our accounts desk.
  • Internal Delivery Limitation: If Sunkiran Dimex is definitively unable to fulfill core deliverables specified in the agreed SOW due to internal capacity constraints or operational limitations.
  • Mutual Scope Reduction: If both parties mutually agree in writing to reduce project scope before subsequent development phases or sprint cycles are provisioned.
Section 03

Non-Refundable Situations & Third-Party Costs

Refunds will strictly not be provided under the following conditions:

  • Initiated or Delivered Work: Once engineering sprints, architectural designs, landing page builds, API integrations, or ad setups have commenced or been submitted for review.
  • Third-Party Ad Spend & Media Costs: Any media budgets paid directly or managed through ad platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok, etc.) are non-recoverable and non-refundable once deployed to ad auctions.
  • Market Performance & Algorithmic Variables: Dissatisfaction due to macroeconomic fluctuations, competitor bid dynamics, audience seasonality, or platform algorithm changes outside agency control.
  • Client-Side Delays & Unresponsiveness: Inactive projects caused by delays in client approvals, creative asset handovers, credentials, or lack of communication exceeding 30 calendar days.
  • Mid-Project Scope Changes: Demands for modifications that deviate fundamentally from the signed Statement of Work without formal change orders.
Important Ad Spend Notice: Media spend consumed on advertising networks is strictly billed by the third-party platforms (Meta, Google). Sunkiran Dimex management fees cover strategic execution and engineering; third-party media costs cannot be refunded.
Section 04

Cancellation Procedure & Notice Periods

Clients may initiate a project cancellation or retainer termination by following our standardized governance protocol:

  • Written Notice Required: All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via registered email to connect@sunkirandimex.com or your dedicated project manager.
  • Monthly Retainer Cancellation: Monthly growth retainers and management contracts require a written notice of 15 business days prior to the upcoming billing renewal cycle.
  • Work-in-Progress Handover: Upon formal cancellation, all completed code modules, design files, ad account access, and documentation produced up to the termination date will be fully packaged and transferred to the client.
Section 05

Milestone Deliveries & Retainer Protection

To guarantee complete transparency and protect both parties, large-scale engineering and custom development projects are partitioned into sequential milestone gates:

Phase 01 Discovery & Blueprint Architecture, wireframes, technical scope, and baseline asset provisioning.
Phase 02 Build & Staging Custom coding, API setup, sandbox testing, and staging environment review.
Phase 03 Production & Launch Live deployment, security checks, tracking verification, and full IP handover.

Payments made for completed and approved milestone phases are non-refundable. Funds held for future, uninitiated phases remain eligible for refund in the event of an authorized project cancellation.

Section 06

Refund Processing & Payout SLA

Once a refund request is reviewed and officially approved by our finance desk, the payout process operates according to strict Service Level Agreements (SLA):

  • Timeline: All approved refunds are processed and remitted within 7 to 10 business days from the formal approval timestamp.
  • Channel: Refunds will be issued directly via the original payment channel (Bank NEFT/RTGS, UPI, Razorpay, Stripe, or Corporate Card).
  • Transaction Fees: Payment gateway processing charges, platform conversion fees, or non-recoverable statutory taxes (GST) may be deducted where mandated by financial regulations.
