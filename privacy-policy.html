1. Privacy Commitment & Scope

Sunkiran Dimex operates as a premier full-cycle technology, performance marketing, and business automation partner based in Pune, India. We recognize that high performance requires high trust. This Privacy Policy governs all interactions with our website (sunkirandimex.com), landing pages, lead generation campaigns, WhatsApp automation integrations, and client engagement portals.

By accessing our website, requesting strategic consultations, or registering for our workshops, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this framework.

2. Information We Collect

We practice strict data minimization: we only collect information necessary to deliver exceptional technology, marketing, and client consultation services.

Personal Identifiers
Full Name, Business Email Address, Phone Number, WhatsApp Contact Handle.
Enterprise Context
Company Name, Industry Sector (Education, Healthcare, Real Estate, etc.), Target Revenue Goals.
Technical Telemetry
Browser type, IP address (anonymized), device viewport, operating system, and session duration.
Inbound Inquiries
Strategic project briefs, workshop registration records, and consultation notes provided voluntarily.

3. Purpose & Processing Principles

All collected personal and business data is processed strictly under legitimate business interest, contract fulfillment, or explicit consent:

  • Strategic Service Delivery: Developing custom software, deploying paid marketing campaigns, and building automated customer pipelines.
  • Direct Communication: Scheduling strategy consultations, delivering workshop links, and providing technical support.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing automated bot submissions and verifying inbound enterprise requests.
  • Product & Campaign Optimization: Aggregating anonymous analytics to optimize conversion pathways.

4. Meta & Third-Party Advertising Governance

As a performance marketing agency running high-volume campaigns across Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Google, we strictly adhere to all platform advertising standards:

  • Meta Conversion API (CAPI): Server-to-server data transmission uses SHA-256 hashed identifiers for privacy-preserving event matching.
  • Instant Lead Forms: Contact info captured via Meta Lead Ads is used exclusively for one-to-one consultation outreach regarding requested services.
  • No Sensitive Data Profiling: We never collect, target, or segment audiences based on sensitive personal categories.
  • Cross-Device Attribution: Aggregated measurement complies with Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and Android Privacy Sandbox guidelines.

5. Data Protection & Security Architecture

We deploy multi-layered security controls to protect client assets from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

Transport Encryption
Strict HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption across all web domains and API endpoints.
Role-Based Access (RBAC)
Access to client marketing data is restricted exclusively to cleared engineering personnel.
Encrypted Storage
Databases utilize AES-256 encryption at rest with automated hourly snapshot redundancy.
Zero Third-Party Sale
Under no circumstances will user records be monetized or transferred to data brokers.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & DPDP)

Regardless of your geographic location, we provide full governance rights over your data in accordance with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and international GDPR standards:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete machine-readable copy of all personal records we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate contact information or corporate details.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget Me): Request the permanent deletion of your contact records and conversation histories.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing dispatches with one click via email or WhatsApp stop command.

8. Data Retention Policy

We maintain data only as long as necessary to fulfill active engagements or satisfy statutory compliance requirements:

  • Inbound Inquiries / Consultations: Retained for 24 months, then automatically archived or purged.
  • Active Client Accounts: Retained for the duration of the active service contract plus statutory tax periods.
  • Workshop Attendees: Retained for 12 months for delivery of workshop assets and recording replays.
